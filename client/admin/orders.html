Orders

Guest and account orders arrive here. Prepare the order, add courier details, dispatch it and confirm delivery.

How do I process an order?

Pending → Processing → Add courier & dispatch → Delivered. Delivery confirms COD payment. For warehouse orders, use Manage warehouse parcels; the order follows all parcels. Use Requests & payments for cancellation, returns or refunds. Administrators and staff with Orders permission manage this workspace.
